MAIN MANUSCRIPT

Figure 1:
Four-stage validation ladder. A reduced-space optimization is connected to the parent full-space fermionic sequence, then to a synthesized qubit circuit, and finally to the measurement protocol. The first connection is exact for all amplitudes only when every implemented parent generator preserves the target subspace, while the later connections introduce independent synthesis and measurement approximations.

Figure 2:
Log-scale energy error versus number of selected ADAPT operators for the canonical Cu benchmark. The projected calculation approaches the 1.6 mEh target, whereas the same-amplitude full-space sequence and its post-projected doublet component remain hundreds of mEh above the target.

Table I:
Comparison of the canonical projected Cu ansatz, its same-amplitude full-space parent sequence, and full-space energy- or fidelity-reoptimized versions of the same 121 generators. Columns report energy error, doublet weight, fidelity to the projected state, and post-projected doublet energy error.

Table II:
Compact representation-faithful Cu ansatze for 18- and 24-qubit fixed Hamiltonians. Rows list active space, operator applications, unique generators, energy error, exact-doublet fidelity, residual norm, and spin expectation value.
