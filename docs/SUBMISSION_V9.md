# Submission v9 - fixed-parent reoptimization and main-text compression

The fixed 121-generator parent sequence was reoptimized directly in full space under hard target-doublet constraints.
The best converged p_D>=0.999 state reaches 8.4815 mEh rather than the 464.730 mEh same-angle error.
Four zero/near-zero starts give 8.4815--8.9734 mEh; p_D>=0.9999 gives 11.2696 mEh from zero amplitudes.
Starts near the original projected amplitudes converge to poorer local minima, so no global optimum is claimed.

Interpretation: the catastrophic same-angle mismatch is primarily a representation/parameter-transfer failure, not proof that the parent sequence is intrinsically useless. A residual fixed-order gap remains in the tested spin-constrained full-space searches.

The main manuscript is shortened from 14 to 8 compiled pages by moving secondary derivations and diagnostics to the SI without removing the central physics, mathematics, chemistry, or reproducibility content.
